# Company Overview

## Company

West Coast Air Conditioning

## Purpose

Provide dependable HVAC service, repairs, maintenance, and equipment replacement while building long-term customer trust.

## Operating Area

Primary service areas include Calaveras County, San Joaquin County, and surrounding communities.

## Core Services

- HVAC service and repair
- Preventive maintenance
- Residential replacement systems
- Light commercial HVAC
- Duct repair and replacement
- Heat pumps and dual-fuel systems
- Indoor air quality solutions

## Growth Goal

Build a stronger operating system that supports growth from under $1 million toward $1–$2 million in annual sales without losing quality, accountability, or customer service.

## Important Branding Compliance

Do not state that West Coast Air Conditioning has been in business since 1967.

The family business history began in 1967, but West Coast Air Conditioning was formed later. Public marketing should use accurate, approved wording.
