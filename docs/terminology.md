# WCAC-OS Terminology

## Gross Revenue

Total money billed to the customer.

## Direct Job Cost

Labor, equipment, materials, permits, subcontractors, and other costs directly tied to a job.

## Gross Profit

Gross revenue minus direct job cost.

## Gross Margin

Gross profit divided by gross revenue.

## Callback

A return visit caused by an unresolved or repeated issue.

## Open Loop

A task, estimate, order, approval, or follow-up that has not been completed.

## Job Closeout

The final review of paperwork, payment, costs, photos, warranty information, and customer follow-up.
