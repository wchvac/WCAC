# Pricing Guidelines

## Principles

1. Price for required gross margin.
2. Include realistic labor hours.
3. Include permits and disposal.
4. Include expected small materials.
5. Include risk for difficult access or unusual conditions.
6. Use written change orders for added work.
7. Do not discount without understanding the margin impact.

## Review Triggers

Management review is required when:

- The job is unusually complex
- The price is below normal range
- The requested discount is significant
- Labor assumptions are uncertain
- Equipment availability may change cost
