# Replacement Sales Process

## Step 1: Qualify

- Confirm system problem
- Confirm ownership and decision-maker
- Confirm timing
- Confirm financing interest
- Confirm comfort concerns

## Step 2: Inspect

- Existing equipment
- Electrical
- Ductwork
- Drainage
- Refrigerant lines
- Thermostat
- Indoor air quality concerns

## Step 3: Present Options

Provide clear good, better, and best choices when appropriate.

## Step 4: Explain Value

Focus on comfort, reliability, efficiency, warranty, installation quality, and long-term cost.

## Step 5: Ask for the Decision

Make the next step clear.

## Step 6: Follow Up

Every unsold estimate must receive a scheduled follow-up.
