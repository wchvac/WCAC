# Estimate Follow-Up Process

## Minimum Follow-Up Sequence

1. Same day: Confirm estimate was received.
2. Two days later: Ask whether the customer has questions.
3. Seven days later: Revisit timing, options, and financing.
4. Fourteen days later: Final active follow-up.
5. Thirty days later: Move to long-term nurture if still undecided.
