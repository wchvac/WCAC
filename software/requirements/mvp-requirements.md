# Minimum Viable Product Requirements

## Users

- Owner
- Office staff
- Technician
- Salesperson
- Manager

## Required Functions

- User login
- Role-based access
- Create and assign tasks
- Set due dates
- Mark tasks complete
- Record estimates
- Record follow-up attempts
- Enter job revenue and direct costs
- Calculate gross profit and gross margin
- Display daily huddle items
- Store procedures and checklists
- Export basic reports

## Future Integrations

- ServiceTitan
- QuickBooks
- Google Calendar
- Gmail
- Text messaging
- Customer review platforms
