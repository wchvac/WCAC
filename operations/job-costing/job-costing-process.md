# Job Costing Process

## Purpose

Determine the real profit on every replacement and major repair job.

## Process

1. Confirm final invoice amount.
2. Add equipment cost.
3. Add material cost.
4. Add permit cost.
5. Add subcontractor cost.
6. Add direct labor cost.
7. Add equipment rental or disposal cost.
8. Calculate total direct job cost.
9. Calculate gross profit.
10. Calculate gross margin.
11. Review any large variance.
12. Record lessons learned.

## Formula

Gross Profit = Revenue - Direct Job Cost

Gross Margin = Gross Profit / Revenue

## Closeout Rule

No replacement job is considered fully closed until job costing is complete.
