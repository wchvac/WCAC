# Service Call Process

## Before Arrival

1. Review customer information.
2. Review previous service history.
3. Confirm complaint.
4. Confirm required tools and common parts.
5. Call or text before arrival when required.

## At the Job

1. Introduce yourself.
2. Confirm the customer's concern.
3. Protect the work area.
4. Diagnose the complete system.
5. Explain findings clearly.
6. Present repair options.
7. Obtain approval before work.
8. Complete repair.
9. Test operation.
10. Clean the work area.

## Before Leaving

1. Explain what was repaired.
2. Explain any remaining concerns.
3. Collect payment or confirm billing.
4. Complete notes and photos.
5. Recommend maintenance or replacement when appropriate.
6. Confirm customer satisfaction.
