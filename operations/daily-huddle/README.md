# Daily Huddle System

## Purpose

Keep the team aligned and prevent important items from falling through the cracks.

## Recommended Length

10 to 15 minutes.

## Daily Huddle Order

1. Safety concerns
2. Today's schedule
3. Customer issues
4. Parts and equipment
5. Estimates waiting
6. Payments and paperwork
7. Open follow-ups
8. Problems needing a decision
9. Wins from yesterday
