# WCAC-OS

West Coast Air Conditioning Operating System

WCAC-OS is the internal operating system for West Coast Air Conditioning. It is designed to make the company easier to run, easier to train, and easier to scale from under $1 million toward $1–$2 million in annual sales.

## Primary Goals

- Improve gross-margin visibility
- Standardize daily huddles
- Create simple step-by-step procedures
- Reduce missed follow-ups
- Improve technician and office training
- Build repeatable sales and service systems
- Prepare the business for future software tools and automation

## Repository Structure

- `docs/` — company-level operating documents
- `operations/` — day-to-day service and office procedures
- `sales/` — sales process, pricing, follow-up, and replacement workflows
- `training/` — technician, office, and sales training
- `software/` — future software requirements, workflows, and data design
- `templates/` — reusable forms, checklists, and worksheets

## How to Use This Repository

1. Start with `docs/company-overview.md`.
2. Review `docs/operating-principles.md`.
3. Use the daily huddle template in `operations/daily-huddle/`.
4. Use the job-costing and gross-margin files after every completed job.
5. Update procedures whenever the company improves a process.

## Operating Rule

Keep every process simple, clear, low-reading, and step-by-step. Avoid unnecessary complexity and memorization.
